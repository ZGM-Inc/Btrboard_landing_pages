The product

A better approach to wall assembly

BTRboard (Better Thermal Resistance) is an insulated wall sheathing product that combines OSB, fire-resistant OSB, or Type X exterior gypsum sheathing with a low-absorption thermoset foam core. By eliminating extra layers and simplifying installation, we help streamline construction while reducing material usage and labour costs for your team.

BTRboard works harder, so you can build smarter. We help you simplify the wall assembly while improving the performance of every {{company}} home.

01 Inert fibreglass facer

03 Substrate layer

Structural sheathing and a solid nailing surface. OSB, FIRE‑X or RESIST.
